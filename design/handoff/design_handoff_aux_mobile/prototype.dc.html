HOW SYNC WORKS
Clock offset is sampled NTP-style, five times, lowest round-trip wins. Drift is corrected on a three-rung ladder — ignore, nudge the playback rate ±2%, or hard-seek. Every reading is written to sync_metrics.

1bThe direction & the system
DIRECTION
Patchbay
Aux is a measuring instrument that happens to be a party. So the app is built like a patchbay: everything on a grid, everything flush left, no rounded corners, no gradients, no glow except the one in the logo. Structure carries the mood — red carries the signal.
Modernist inverted to night, because the product is used at 1am. Album art becomes a typographic block until real artwork lands, so the layout survives any cover, including ugly ones.
RED IS RESERVED
One colour means live, playing, joinable, in sync, on aux. It appears nowhere else — no decorative tint, no selected form row. That is why the Feed is scannable.

THE DRIFT LADDER, MADE VISIBLE
±40ms
Locked. Solid red mark, solid avatar.
±220ms
Nudging at ±2%. Mark goes white — no red.
BEYOND
Hard seek. Mark greys out and slides back.
Losing sync is shown by losing the red, not by turning amber. The palette stays mono and the signal stays honest.
