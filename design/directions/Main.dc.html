direction
Four visual worlds for the same product. Each one draws the Session screen — the party itself — because that is where Aux either feels like something or doesn’t. Tell me a letter and I’ll build the whole app in it.
A Marquee
Gig-poster typography. The track title is the interface — set enormous, art kept small. Risograph duotone on black.
Trade-off: loud and memorable, but long track titles fight the layout.
B Tape Deck
Physical hi-fi. Brushed metal, amber meters, transport keys you can almost feel. Sync reads as a needle.
Trade-off: warm and tactile; skeuomorphism dates faster than flat.
C Signal
Makes the invisible visible. One playhead line runs through everyone’s avatar — you can literally see you are together.
Trade-off: the only one that designs the sync itself; reads technical, less cosy.
D Afterglow
The artwork floods the room. Huge bloom, almost no chrome, everything soft. Closest to 1am on a sofa.
Trade-off: gorgeous with good art, mushy with bad art — and contrast needs care.
Constant across all four
Dark. One reserved “live” colour. Album art survives being ugly. 44px targets, 16px body, 4.5:1 contrast.
What I need from you
A letter. Or take one direction’s type and another’s colour — mixing is fine and often better than picking.
